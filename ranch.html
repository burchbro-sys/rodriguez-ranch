Twenty Acres. One Way of Life.

The future Rodriguez Ranch is envisioned as a working property where animals, veterinary medicine, food production, plants, land, and family operate together rather than separately.

Enter the Ranch

The Big Picture

Built to Work Together.

Rodriguez Ranch is planned around roughly twenty acres of usable land, with each area serving a purpose within the larger ranch ecosystem.

The veterinary center can operate from a dedicated portion of the property while the rest of the land supports pastures, livestock, poultry, gardens, food production, greenhouse growing, rescue work, and future ranch businesses.

The goal is not simply to own land. The goal is to build a place capable of producing food, caring for animals, supporting businesses, and creating a more self-sufficient way of life.
