Life on Rodriguez Ranch

Built Around Animals.

Horses in the pasture. Dogs following along. Goats and sheep grazing. Chickens scratching through the grass. Ducks moving between land and water.

The animals aren't decoration for Rodriguez Ranch. They're part of what gives the place its purpose.

Meet the Ranch

The Philosophy

Give Them Space to Be Animals.

Rodriguez Ranch is envisioned around the needs of the animals living there, rather than fitting animals into whatever space happens to be available.

Pastures, shelter, fencing, water, veterinary care, enrichment, and species-appropriate environments all become part of the property plan from the beginning.

Some animals may eventually contribute to agriculture. Others may be companions, working animals, rescues, or simply permanent residents of the ranch.
